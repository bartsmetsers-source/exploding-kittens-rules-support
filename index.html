Can a Nope stop an Exploding Kitten?

139

No. A Nope can only cancel card effects. It cannot be played on an Exploding Kitten or a Defuse card.

140
141 142
143

Do Attack cards stack?

144

Yes! If player A plays an Attack, player B must take 2 turns. If player B also plays an Attack, player C must take 4 turns.

146 147
148

Where do I put the Exploding Kitten after using a Defuse?

149

You decide — secretly. You can place it anywhere in the deck: top, bottom, or anywhere in between. You may also bluff about where you put it.

150
151 152
153

Can a Nope be Noped?

154

Yes! If you play a Nope and someone plays a Nope on your Nope, your Nope is cancelled and the original card effect goes through.

155
156 157
158

Is there a hand limit?

159

No. You can hold as many cards as you like. There is no maximum hand size.

160
161 162
163

Am I allowed to lie?

164

Yes! You may lie about what you saw with See the Future, where you placed the Exploding Kitten, and how many Defuse cards you have.

165
166 167
168

How many Exploding Kittens are in the deck?

169

Always the number of players minus one. With 4 players there are 3 Exploding Kittens in the deck.
